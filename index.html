Die App speichert deine Eingaben (z. B. Berechtigungen

  • Benachrichtigungen: für Erinnerungen an deine Fristen. Sie werden lokal auf dem Gerät geplant.

Beim Scannen von Kassenbons und Verträgen wird der Text mit Google ML Kit direkt auf deinem Gerät erkannt. Bilder und erkannter Text verlassen das Gerät nicht.

Die automatische Android-Cloud-Sicherung ist für DeadlineGuard abgeschaltet. Wenn du selbst ein Backup erstellst, wird es mit einem von dir gewählten Passwort verschlüsselt (AES-256). Du entscheidest, wo du die Datei speicherst (z. B. Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Computer). Für diesen Speicherort gelten die Datenschutzbestimmungen des jeweiligen Anbieters. Ohne dein Passwort kann niemand – auch wir nicht – das Backup öffnen.

Data the app processes

The app stores what you enter (e.g. contract and purchase details, notes, your name and address for cancellation letters) and attached photos and PDFs in its private storage on your device. We have no access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for deadline reminders, scheduled locally on the device.
  • Camera / photos / files: only when you take or choose a picture of a receipt or contract.
  • Fingerprint / face / device PIN: only for the optional app lock. The operating system performs the check; the app receives no biometric data.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the device restarts.

Text recognition

When you scan receipts or contracts, the text is recognised on your device using Google ML Kit. Images and recognised text never leave the device.

Backups

Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for DeadlineGuard. If you create a backup yourself, it is encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256). You decide where to store the file (e.g. Google Drive, iCloud Drive, a computer); the privacy policy of that provider applies there. Without your password nobody – including us – can open the backup.

In-app purchase

Buying DeadlineGuard Pro is handled entirely by Google Play (or the App Store). We receive no payment details. Google's or Apple's privacy policy applies.

Your rights

As we do not process any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete – you are always in full control of your data in the app. You can contact us with any questions and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
